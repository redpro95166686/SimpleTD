📖 TUTORIAL

🎯 Basics

▼

Welcome to SIMPLE TD! Your goal is to build towers along a path to defeat waves of enemies before they reach the end.

💡 Quick Start: Click on any grass tile to open the tower selector, then choose a tower to build. Your first tower starts the game!
🖱️ Try it now: Click a grass tile in the game to build your first tower.

⌨️ Controls

▼

🖱️ Mouse – click a grass tile to build, click a tower to upgrade/sell.

⌨️ Keyboard – P to pause/resume, Space to speed up (hold).

💡 Tip: Holding Space temporarily speeds up the game – great for fast-forwarding through early waves!
⌨️ Try it: Press P in the game to pause, and Space to boost speed.

📈 Progression

▼

Coins 🪙 – earned from kills and wave bonuses. Used to build and upgrade towers.

Gems 💎 – earned from wave completions. Used in the Shop to unlock towers and increase max levels.

Shards 🔮 – earned every 10 levels. Used in the Shop's Skill Tree to unlock tower upgrades and knowledge.

Level & Prestige – killing enemies gives XP. Reach level 410 to start earning Prestige, the ultimate endgame.

💡 Tip: Prestige unlocks special gold/green themes at levels 500 and 750!

💡 Strategy

▼

Early game: Build Archers near the start of the path.

Mid game: Add Farms for passive income. Upgrade your best towers.

Late game: Use Bombers and Mages for crowd control, Snipers for boss damage.

Heroes: Place your hero early to maximize their leveling potential.

Gems: Unlock new towers and increase max tower levels in the Shop.

Shards: Invest in tower upgrades and knowledge in the Skill Tree.
