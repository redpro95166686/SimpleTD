🎓 Learn to Play

🎯 Basics

Goal: Defend your base by building towers along a path to stop enemies from reaching the end. Each enemy that slips through costs you a life. Lose all lives and it's game over!

Coins 🪙 – earned by killing enemies and completing waves. Used to build and upgrade towers.

Gems 💎 – earned every wave. Used in the Shop to unlock new towers, increase max levels, buy permanent upgrades, and unlock heroes.

Shards 🔮 – earned every 10 player levels. Used in the Skill Tree for tower upgrades, hero upgrades, and powerful Knowledge upgrades.

💡 Tip: Your first tower starts the game with a scout wave – watch him walk the path to see where enemies will go!

🏰 Towers

Place towers on grass tiles (not on the path). Each tower has unique strengths:

🏹
Archer
Cheap, reliable single‑target damage.
💣
Bomber
Drops bombs on the path – splash damage.
☠️
Venom
Aura that poisons nearby enemies.
🎯
Dart
Fast, high critical hit chance.
🌾
Farm
Generates passive income over time.
🔭
Sniper
Infinite range, massive damage – great for bosses.
🧙
Mage
Chain lightning that bounces between enemies.

Placing: Click a tower icon in the sidebar to select it, then click a grass tile. You can also drag towers directly onto the map.

Upgrading: Click a built tower to open its popup – spend coins to increase its level, boosting damage, range, and fire rate.

💡 Strategy: Mix tower types! Archers for early waves, Farms for economy, Bombers for crowds, Snipers for bosses.

🦸 Heroes

Heroes are powerful units that provide unique abilities and buffs. You can unlock them with gems in the Shop.

🕷️
Clacks
Throws mini bombs (area damage). Boosts tower fire rate.
🕸️
Spoder
Shoots in 8 directions, applies poison, gives a global discount.
🎯
Mini Fun
Fires in a chosen direction continuously – can be rotated.
🌳
Trae
Tree that generates gold and buffs farms; levels up with farms placed.

Placing: Once unlocked, select a hero from the sidebar (🦸 icon) and click a grass tile. You can only place each hero once per game (unless Unlimited Heroes is enabled).

Leveling: Heroes gain levels by dealing damage – each level improves their abilities. Place them early so they have time to grow!

💡 Tip: Trae is excellent for farming strategies. Clacks works well with any tower setup.

👾 Enemies & Bosses

Enemies come in waves. Each has different stats:

  • 🟢 Sprout – Weak, numerous.
  • 🔵 Frostling – Tanky, slow.
  • 🟠 Charger – Fast, fragile.
  • 🔴 Brute – High HP, tough.
  • 🟣 Shade – Elusive, dodges attacks.

Bosses appear at waves 40, 80, and 100:

  • 🐕 Cerberus – Wave 40
  • 🐘 Behemoth – Wave 80
  • 👑 Titan – Wave 100

After wave 100, mini‑bosses can appear randomly.

💡 Tip: Check the Bestiary (📖) for detailed stats and combat tips against each enemy.

⚔️ Difficulty & Rogue‑like

There are 7 difficulty tiers (Easy, Normal, Hard, Challenger, Death, Hell, Primal). Each is harder and must be unlocked by reaching specific waves on the previous difficulty.

Rogue‑like Mode becomes available once you unlock all 7 difficulties. In this mode:

  • You start with only the Archer tower – you unlock more as you go.
  • Every 5 waves, you choose 1 of 3 random upgrades (tower unlocks, buffs, hero unlocks, etc.).
  • Difficulty is fixed to Normal.
  • Win by reaching Wave 100!
💡 Tip: Rogue‑like is a great way to test your adaptability. Choose upgrades that complement your current towers.

🏪 Shop & Skill Tree

Shop (Gems 💎)

  • Unlock new towers (Bomber, Venom, Dart, Farm, Sniper, Mage).
  • Increase a tower's max level (costs increasing gems).
  • Buy permanent upgrades: Extra Starting Cash (+200 coins) and Fortifications (+5 lives).
  • Unlock heroes with gems.

Skill Tree (Shards 🔮)

  • Spend shards on tower‑specific upgrades (3 per tower).
  • Spend shards on hero upgrades (3 per hero).
  • Spend shards on Knowledge – powerful global upgrades (Archery, Serpants, Heroes Chant, Heroes Wallet).
💡 Tip: Prioritize upgrades that match your playstyle. Archer upgrades are great early, Farm upgrades boost income, Knowledge unlocks global bonuses.

⌨️ Controls

🖱️ Mouse / Touch

  • Select a tower: Click a tower icon in the sidebar (or drag it).
  • Place a tower: Click a grass tile on the map – the selected tower will be built.
  • Drag & drop: Drag a tower icon from the sidebar directly onto the map.
  • Manage towers: Click a built tower to upgrade or sell it.
  • Game menu: Click the ☰ (hamburger) button in the top‑left.

⌨️ Keyboard

  • P – Pause / Resume
  • Space – Hold to speed up the game (Turbo)
  • ← / → – Seek in Music player
  • ↑ / ↓ – Volume control in Music player

💡 Strategy Guide

Early game (Waves 1–15): Build 2–3 Archers near the start, save for a Farm to generate income, and upgrade your best Archer.

Mid game (Waves 16–50): Add 2–3 Farms, unlock Bombers or Venom for crowd control, place your hero early, and save gems for tower unlocks.

Late game (Waves 51+): Build Snipers for bosses, use Mages for groups, max level your key towers, and invest in Skill Tree upgrades.

Rogue‑like: Prioritize Farm unlocks early, choose buffs that complement your towers, and grab hero unlocks when they appear.

💡 Tip: Check the Bestiary to learn enemy weaknesses, and the Achievements page for long‑term goals!
