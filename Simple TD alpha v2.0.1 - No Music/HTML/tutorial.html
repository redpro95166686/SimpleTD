📖 TUTORIAL

🎯 Basics

▼

Welcome to SIMPLE TD! Your goal is to build towers along a path to defeat waves of enemies before they reach the end.

Each enemy that reaches the end costs you a life. Lose all lives and it's game over!

💡 Quick Start: Select a tower from the sidebar (click or drag), then click a grass tile to build it. Your first tower starts the game with a scout wave!
🖱️ Try it now: Click a tower icon in the sidebar → click a grass tile to place it.

🎲 Rogue‑like Mode New Rogue

▼

Rogue‑like Mode is a fresh, run‑based challenge where every run starts from scratch. Only the Archer tower is available at the start!

⚠️ Unlock Requirement: All 7 difficulties must be unlocked before Rogue‑like Mode appears in the Play menu.

How it works:

  • Every run starts fresh – no outside progress carries over.
  • You start with only the Archer tower.
  • Every 5 waves, you get to choose 1 of 3 random upgrades.
  • Upgrades include: unlock new towers, increase max level, tower buffs, global buffs, or rare hero unlocks.
  • Difficulty is fixed to NORMAL.
  • Win by reaching Wave 100!
💡 Strategy: Choose upgrades that complement your current towers. Unlocking Farms early gives you income, while tower buffs make your defenses stronger. Heroes are rare – grab them when they appear!
🏆 Achievements: Rogue‑like has its own set of achievements – track your progress in the Achievements page!

🏪 Shop & Skill Tree Core

▼

Shop – Gems 💎

  • Unlock new towers (Bomber, Venom, Dart, Farm, Sniper, Mage).
  • Increase a tower's max level (costs increasing gems).
  • Buy permanent upgrades: Extra Starting Cash (+200 coins) and Fortifications (+5 lives).
  • Unlock Heroes with gems.

Skill Tree – Shards 🔮

  • Earn shards every 10 player levels.
  • Spend shards on tower‑specific upgrades (3 per tower).
  • Spend shards on Hero upgrades (3 per hero).
  • Spend shards on Knowledge – powerful global upgrades (Archery, Serpants, Heroes Chant, Heroes Wallet).
💡 Tip: Prioritize upgrades that match your playstyle. Archer upgrades are great early, Farm upgrades boost income, and Knowledge unlocks powerful global bonuses.

📈 Progression

▼

Coins 🪙 – earned from kills and wave bonuses. Used to build and upgrade towers.

Gems 💎 – earned from wave completions. Used in the Shop to unlock towers, increase max levels, buy permanent upgrades, and unlock heroes.

Shards 🔮 – earned every 10 player levels. Used in the Shop's Skill Tree for tower upgrades, hero upgrades, and Knowledge.

Level & Prestige – killing enemies gives XP. Reach level 410 to start earning Prestige, the ultimate endgame. Prestige unlocks special themes at levels 500 and 750!

💡 Tip: Prestige is the long‑term goal. Each prestige level is a testament to your dedication!

💡 Strategy Guide

▼

Early game (Waves 1–15):

  • Build 2–3 Archers near the start of the path.
  • Save coins for a Farm to start generating passive income.
  • Upgrade your best Archer when possible.

Mid game (Waves 16–50):

  • Add 2–3 Farms for steady income.
  • Unlock Bombers or Venom for crowd control.
  • Place your hero early to start leveling them.
  • Save gems for tower unlocks in the Shop.

Late game (Waves 51+):

  • Build Snipers for boss damage (infinite range).
  • Use Mages for chain lightning on groups.
  • Max level your key towers.
  • Invest in Skill Tree upgrades for permanent power.

Rogue‑like strategy:

  • Prioritize Farm unlocks early – income is crucial.
  • Choose tower buffs that complement your existing towers.
  • Hero unlocks are rare – grab them when you see them!
  • Don't be afraid to restart if you get unlucky upgrades – each run is a learning experience.
💡 Tip: Check the Bestiary to learn enemy weaknesses, and the Achievements page for long‑term goals!
